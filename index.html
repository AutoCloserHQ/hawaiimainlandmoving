08 / Before you go

A few things worth knowing.

Who will handle my move?

HawaiiMainlandMoving.com is operated by ModelMoving, a quote request and matching platform. A selected moving provider confirms availability, pricing, service terms and transportation arrangements.

Can I move from the mainland to Hawaii?

Yes. Choose Mainland to Hawaii in the form and enter your mainland origin and destination island or community.

How long will my move take?

Timing depends on the provider, route, shipment and service plan. Ask about the full sequence from pickup through ocean transport and final delivery, not only the sailing stage.

Can I include a car or request packing?

Yes. Add those needs in the Move Details step. The provider confirms whether they are included in its proposal or require separate arrangements.

Do you give instant prices?

No. The cost explorer explains factors that may influence pricing. Actual quotes come from providers after reviewing the move.
