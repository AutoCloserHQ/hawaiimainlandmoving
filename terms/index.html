Website Terms

Terms for using HawaiiMainlandMoving.com planning resources and quote requests, including ModelMoving’s role and provider responsibilities.

Using this website

HawaiiMainlandMoving.com is a moving-information and quote-request resource operated by ModelMoving. These terms describe use of the website. Updated September 5, 2026.

Use the site for legitimate planning and requests you are authorized to make. Provide accurate details and do not submit another person’s contact information without authorization. Do not misuse the form, interfere with the site or submit unlawful content.

Quote requests are not bookings

Submitting the form sends a request for review and possible matching with selected moving partners based on route, timing, availability and move details. It does not create a transportation contract, reserve a date or guarantee a price, provider, response time or number of quotes.

ModelMoving operates the request and matching platform. Final pricing, availability, service terms and transportation arrangements are provided by the selected moving provider.

Information and estimates

The guides and interactive tools provide general planning information. Cost factors do not calculate an actual quote. Timeline reminders are preparation suggestions, not guaranteed pickup, sailing or delivery schedules.

Actual requirements depend on the provider, route, shipment and service plan. Confirm current acceptance rules, preparation instructions, access needs, schedules and service terms directly with the provider before booking or relying on an arrangement.

Provider agreements and outside links

Review the selected provider’s written agreement, payment and cancellation terms, protection provisions and claims process. This site does not promise carrier assets, particular ocean arrangements or coverage for loss or damage.

Links to independent resources are provided for context. They do not establish a partnership or incorporate an outside provider’s service into your request. Third-party websites and services have their own terms and privacy practices.

Privacy and changes

See the privacy notice for information submitted with requests and the use of attribution and analytics. The consent shown with the form describes contact about your request.

Website content and these terms may change. For questions about the website, use the contact options at ModelMoving. For questions about a booked service, contact the provider responsible for that agreement.
