About HawaiiMainlandMoving.com

A Hawaii-mainland planning resource operated by ModelMoving, with clear logistics guidance and moving quote request matching.

A clearer starting point for a Pacific move

Moving a home between Hawaii and the mainland involves more than choosing two cities. It means deciding what travels, preparing the pickup property, understanding ocean and handling stages, and coordinating a home at the other end.

HawaiiMainlandMoving.com brings those questions together. The goal is to help you understand your move well enough to request useful options and evaluate what a provider proposes.

Who operates this site

The site is operated by ModelMoving, a moving quote request and matching platform that helps connect customers with selected moving partners based on route, timing, availability and move details.

We describe that role openly. This website does not present itself as the owner of a transportation fleet, ship, warehouse or moving crew. Final pricing, availability, transportation arrangements and service terms come from the selected moving provider.

What you can do here

  • Plan a move in either direction between Hawaii and the mainland.
  • Read island-specific preparation guidance for Oahu, Maui and the Big Island.
  • Explore the stages that may connect pickup to final delivery.
  • Identify cost factors and vehicle-planning questions.
  • Submit your move details for quote-request matching.

Kauai and custom Hawaii locations can be entered in the request form. A provider must confirm the actual coverage and service plan.

How to use our guidance

Use these pages as a preparation resource. They intentionally avoid fixed transit promises, invented quote numbers and unsupported claims about providers. Your actual shipment, access, schedule and service agreement determine what is possible.

Where an independent provider resource is linked, it is for additional context. A reference link is not a claim of partnership, endorsement or an offer of that provider’s service through this website.

Take the next useful step

Start with the Hawaii-to-mainland guide or the mainland-to-Hawaii guide. If you already know your route, share the basics in the three-step request.
